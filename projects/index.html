Projects & Notes

Projects

  • Burnside Sampler

    An interactive browser visualizer and verified prime-field implementation of Burnside sampling on the symmetric group.

    • Interactive demo

  • Modular Reduction

    A Python and Sage research package for modular reduction of complex representations of finite reductive groups.

  • braidmod

    A machine-learning project that uses Burau matrices and model confusion to probe the kernel of the reduced Burau representation in B₄ mod p.

Talks & Notes

  • Animated 3-Manifolds

    Under the supervision of Dror Bar-Natan in 2017, I used computer graphics and animation to create a visual companion to Allen Hatcher’s book Notes on 3-Manifold Topology.

  • Kazhdan-Laumon Poster

    A poster describing my work on Kazhdan-Laumon categories from the Canada-USA-Mexico representation theory conference in Montreal in August 2023.

  • Quantum Groups Notes

    Notes from the talks I gave on the proof of a PBW-type theorem for quantum groups via a quantum braid group action at the Quantum Groups Seminar at IAS.

Burnside Sampler

An interactive browser visualizer and verified prime-field implementation of Burnside sampling on the symmetric group.

The Burnside sampler is an interactive browser visualizer and verified prime-field implementation of Burnside sampling on the symmetric group. It accompanies my paper with Persi Diaconis, Markov chains on Weyl groups from the geometry of the flag variety.

Try the interactive sampler to adjust the field characteristic and starting permutation and explore the resulting Markov chain.

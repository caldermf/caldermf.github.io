The **Burnside sampler** is an interactive browser visualizer and verified prime-field implementation of Burnside sampling on the symmetric group. It accompanies my paper with Persi Diaconis, [*Markov chains on Weyl groups from the geometry of the flag variety*]({{ '/publications/markovchains/' | relative_url }}).

[Try the interactive sampler]({{ '/burnside-sampler/web/' | relative_url }}) to adjust the field characteristic and starting permutation and explore the resulting Markov chain.

[Source code and documentation](https://github.com/caldermf/burnside-sampler).

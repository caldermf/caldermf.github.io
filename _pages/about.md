I am a mathematician and research scientist currently working on machine learning, with a focus on reasoning in language models and applications to mathematical, scientific, and medical research.

From 2019 to 2024, I completed my PhD in math at <abbr>MIT</abbr> under the supervision of Roman Bezrukavnikov. My mathematical background and interests lie at the intersection of algebra, geometry, and combinatorics, particularly in geometric representation theory.

From 2024 to 2025, I was a Szegő Assistant Professor at Stanford University, and from 2025 to 2026, I was a Gibbs Assistant Professor at Yale University.

On [my GitHub page](https://github.com/caldermf) you can find my technical projects, most of which I developed as research tools related to the papers listed here.
